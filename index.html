THE OPPORTUNITY

Built for what happens
between your rides.

Technical riding skills are learned on the bike. Physical preparation often happens elsewhere. The product aims to connect those two worlds without replacing qualified coaching.

01 / PLAN
↗

Progressive conditioning

Strength, endurance and mobility sessions organized around riding goals, season phase, equipment and training availability.

02 / LEARN
✳

Coach-informed guidance

Exercise explanations and educational materials developed with qualified coaches, supported by an AI assistant.

03 / ADAPT
⌁

Measured progress

Use completed training sessions and user feedback to support progress tracking and controlled adjustments to future plans.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Human expertise at the core.

01

Expert-led methodology

Training prescriptions should follow coach-reviewed rules. AI helps explain plans, rather than inventing unrestricted training loads.

02

Privacy by design

The proposed architecture separates Garmin-derived activity data from external AI providers unless properly authorized. Access will require user permission.

03

Honest product scope

Currently in development. No subscriber service, app registration, or Garmin data collection is operated through this website.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Independent idea.
Serious ambition.

MTB Training Lab is a working-title concept developed by Ziemowit Słomian, a sole proprietor based in Poland, with a background in AI and software development.

The vision is to build a specialized training platform and explore collaborations with mountain bike instructors and physical conditioning professionals. No specific school or coaching partnership is being represented as concluded.
