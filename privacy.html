policy.

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy applies only to the informational website. The training application and Garmin integration are not live.

1. Who operates this website?

The data controller for this informational website is Ziemowit Słomian, an independent sole proprietor based in Poland.

For questions about your personal data, write to zslomian@gmail.com.

2. What this website does today

This is a static, pre-launch project website. It does not provide user registration, subscriptions, payments, connected fitness accounts or a contact form.

We do not currently collect Garmin activity data, health metrics, location traces or workout files through this website. The proposed application and Garmin API integration are not yet operational.

3. What personal information may be processed?

Information you choose to send: if you email us, we will process your email address, the content of your message and any other information you voluntarily provide, solely to communicate with you and respond to your inquiry.

Technical website access: the site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information such as IP addresses and request logs when delivering the site, under its own applicable policies.

We have not added our own analytics scripts, advertising pixels or tracking cookies to this website.

4. Why we process information

We respond to inquiries on the basis of our legitimate interest in communicating with people interested in the project (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or, where appropriate, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

We do not sell personal information or use inquiries for behavioral advertising.

5. Service providers and international transfers

GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts and delivers this informational website. Please see GitHub's privacy statement for information about GitHub's processing.

Email service: incoming messages are handled by the email provider of the published contact address. Access and storage may involve service providers operating outside the European Economic Area, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

We do not send Garmin device-sourced data to any external AI provider through this website.

6. How long information is retained

We keep correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry and related follow-up, unless a longer period is required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable GDPR conditions, you may request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or data portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise your rights, email zslomian@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

8. External links

Links to LinkedIn and other third-party websites open services with their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for the content or privacy policies of those services.

9. Changes before product launch

If the training application launches or Garmin Connect integration becomes available, we will publish a separate or expanded notice before collecting user accounts or training data. That notice will describe the relevant activity data, purposes, legal bases, retention, recipients, security and user controls.

This current policy should not be interpreted as a privacy policy for a functioning training application.
